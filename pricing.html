Questions

The things owners ask first.

What happens during the pilot?
We set Misafir up on one venue and run it for a week — watching reviews and messages, drafting replies, and sending you a brief. You see exactly what it produces on your real guests before any money or commitment changes hands.
Will it ever reply to a guest without me seeing it?
No. Every reply, post and message is a draft until a person approves it. The human gate is built in and never comes off.
How does hotel (per-room) pricing work?
Restaurants pay a flat monthly rate per venue. Hotels pay per room per month — £3 / £5 / £7 depending on the tier — with a monthly minimum so small properties and large ones are both fairly priced. Use the toggle above to compare. Final pricing also depends on your market and currency (GBP, EUR or TRY); we'll quote clearly before you decide.
Which languages can it handle?
Turkish, English, German and many more out of the box — it replies in whatever language the guest wrote in. Multilingual drafting is the core of what Misafir does.
Do I need to change my booking system or tools?
No rip-and-replace. Misafir reads the channels you already run. For reservations we scope your existing setup with you first.
What about my guests' data?
We build with EU GDPR and Turkey's KVKK in mind — a clear basis for processing and care over what's stored and where. We'll walk through it before the pilot.
Am I locked into a contract?
No long contracts. You start with a pilot and stay because it's pulling its weight.
