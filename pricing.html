Questions

The things owners ask first.

What happens during the pilot?
We set Misafir up on one of your venues and run it for a week — watching reviews and messages, drafting replies in the right language, and sending you a brief. You see exactly what it produces on your real guests before any money or commitment changes hands.
Will it ever reply to a guest without me seeing it?
No. Every reply, post, and message is a draft until a person approves it. The human gate is built into the product and never comes off — it's both the safety mechanism and the reason everything still sounds like your house.
Are these the final prices?
They're a starting point. Final pricing depends on your market and currency (GBP, EUR or TRY) and whether you're a single venue or a group — per-venue versus a flat group rate. We'll quote clearly before you decide.
Which languages can it handle?
Turkish, English, German, and many more out of the box — it replies in whatever language the guest wrote in. Multilingual drafting is the core of what Misafir does, not an add-on.
Do I need to change my booking system or tools?
No rip-and-replace. Misafir reads the channels you already run — Google, WhatsApp Business, email, Instagram. For reservations we scope your existing setup (POS, an OpenTable-style tool, or WhatsApp) with you first, so nothing is over-promised.
What about my guests' data and privacy?
Handling reviews and messages means handling personal data, so we build with EU GDPR and Turkey's KVKK in mind — a clear basis for processing and care over what's stored and where. We'll walk through it with you before the pilot. (We'll always recommend you get your own legal sign-off too.)
Am I locked into a contract?
No long contracts. You start with a pilot and stay because it's pulling its weight. If it isn't, you walk away having lost nothing.
