  • UK / EU / rest of world: Seam Studio Ltd, registered in England & Wales, company number [UK company number], registered office [UK registered address], London, United Kingdom.

2. Scope

This policy covers personal data we collect through the Misafir website — mainly when you request a pilot, start onboarding, or otherwise contact us. Where Misafir processes your guests' data as part of providing the service to a venue, that is covered by section 5 and a separate data processing agreement.

3. What data we collect

Information you give us

Through our pilot/onboarding/contact forms we collect the details you enter, which may include your name, email, venue or group name, number of venues, the tier you're interested in, and your message. Please don't include sensitive information in free-text fields.

Information collected automatically

Our site is static with no advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting and form providers process limited technical data (IP address, browser/device type, timestamps) in server logs to deliver the site and prevent abuse. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address on load (see Cookies).

5. Your guests' data

When a venue uses Misafir, the service reads guest messages and reviews and drafts replies. In that context the venue is the controller of its guests' data and Misafir acts as a processor, handling that data only on the venue's documented instructions under a data processing agreement. Misafir is draft-first and human-gated: nothing is sent to a guest without the venue's approval. We build with data minimisation and the UK GDPR / EU GDPR / KVKK in mind, and we agree data handling with each venue before a pilot begins.

7. International transfers

Some providers are outside the UK/EEA (notably the USA). We rely on an appropriate safeguard — the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision (including the UK/EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider participates).

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period after — typically up to 24 months from last contact — unless the law requires longer. Guest data processed for a venue is retained per that venue's agreement and deleted or returned on request.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, contact us (section 12). You can also complain to a regulator — in the UK the ICO (ico.org.uk); in Türkiye the KVKK Kurumu.

10. Turkey — KVKK

If you're in Türkiye, our processing is also governed by Law No. 6698 (KVKK). See our dedicated KVKK Aydınlatma Metni.

11. Cookies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Details are in our Cookie & Tracking Notice.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, email [privacy@seamuk.com] or use our contact form. Our full terms are in the Terms of Use.
