What it does

Start with the essentials. Grow into the rest.

Most venues begin with reviews and messages — the daily noise that's easy to let slip. The rest layers on when you're ready.

Start here

Reviews & reputation

  • Watches new Google reviews across every venue, daily.
  • Drafts an on-brand reply in the reviewer's language.
  • Flags 1–2★ reviews as urgent so none slip past.
  • Nudges happy guests to leave a review after they visit.
Start here

Guest messages

  • Reads and drafts replies to WhatsApp Business messages.
  • Triages email enquiries: booking, event, press, supplier.
  • Answers the repeat questions — hours, parking, dietary.
  • Flags the big ones: large groups, journalists, VIPs.

Reservations & guests

  • Confirmations and day-before reminders to cut no-shows.
  • Follow-ups after no-shows and cancellations.
  • Post-visit thank-you that asks for a review.
  • Remembers birthdays and anniversaries for VIP touches.

Social & content

  • Instagram captions in Turkish and English from one photo.
  • A weekly content calendar so it never goes quiet.
  • Event and seasonal-menu announcements, drafted.
  • Replies to Instagram comments and DMs.

Owner's brief

  • A weekly read on every venue, side by side.
  • The themes guests keep raising, good and bad.
  • Early warning when a rating starts to slip.
  • A short list of what's actually worth acting on.

Market watch

  • Keeps an eye on nearby venues' new reviews.
  • Flags new openings and notable events close by.
  • Tracks how you stack up on rating over time.
  • Surfaces it all in the same weekly brief.
