How it works

Watch, draft, and wait for your yes.

There's no dashboard to babysit and no robot let loose on your guests. Misafir works quietly in the background and only ever asks one thing of you: a quick approval.

The loop

Four steps, repeated all day.

The same proven pattern behind our automation work — pointed at the things a busy venue never gets to.

Watch

It notices

New Google review, a WhatsApp message, an email enquiry — across all your venues, checked daily or twice a day in peak season.

Read

It understands

What language is this in? Is it a complaint, a booking, press, a supplier? How urgent? It sorts the noise before you ever see it.

Draft

It writes

A reply in the guest's own language and your house tone — ready to send, not a rough translation you have to fix.

Gate

You approve

Everything lands as a draft. You glance, tweak if you like, and approve. Nothing is published in your name until you do.

A week in the life

What actually lands in your inbox.

No new app to learn. It comes to you — as briefs and drafts you can act on in minutes.

Every morning

  • A short brief: what needs a reply, what's urgent, what's just good to know.
  • Drafted replies attached — approve or tweak in a tap.
  • Today's bookings, events, and special requests for the team.

Through the day

  • A 1★ review comes in → flagged urgent, reply already drafted.
  • A party-of-12 WhatsApp → triaged, answer ready in their language.
  • A press enquiry → pushed to the top, not lost under suppliers.

Mid-week

  • A content calendar so the feed never goes quiet.
  • Captions in Turkish and English from a single photo.
  • Post-visit thank-yous that quietly ask happy guests for a review.
The centerpiece

Every Sunday night

  • The owner's brief: ratings, themes, and no-show rates per venue.
  • All three venues side by side — who's up, who needs a look.
  • A trend alert before a slipping rating becomes a problem.
  • A short list of what's actually worth your time this week.

Where it plugs in

It meets you where your guests already are.

No rip-and-replace. Misafir reads the channels you already run, and we scope anything custom with you before promising it.

Google & reviews

Reads new reviews across every venue. For the paid rollout, we set up replies posted straight back to your Google Business Profile.

WhatsApp & email

Connects to your WhatsApp Business account and shared mailbox to read incoming messages and draft the replies.

Your booking system

POS, an OpenTable-style tool, or WhatsApp — we scope your setup before the reservations module goes live, so nothing's over-promised.

Trust & data care

Your guests' words stay your guests' words.

Handling reviews and messages means handling personal data. We treat that seriously, and design the human gate in from the start.

The human gate is the product

  • Nothing is posted, sent, or published without a person's sign-off.
  • It's both the safety mechanism and the reason the work still sounds like you.
  • You can edit any draft before it goes — it learns your tone over time.

GDPR & KVKK aware

  • Built with EU GDPR and Turkey's KVKK in mind from day one.
  • A clear basis for processing, and care over what's stored and where.
  • We'll walk through data handling with you before the pilot starts.
